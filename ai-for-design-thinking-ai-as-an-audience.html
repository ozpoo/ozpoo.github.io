AI For Design Thinking
AI as an Audience

AI For Design Thinking: A collection of small experiments for using AI as part of the design process — it doesn’t have to make the thing, it can help you see the thing.

Use AI to step outside your own perspective.

You have been looking at your design for hours.

You know what you meant.

Your audience doesn’t.

Give AI your work and ask it to respond as someone who has never seen the project before.

Don’t explain your intentions first.

Instead, provide only what your audience would reasonably have:

  • the design
  • the context
  • the situation
  • the task

Then ask:

What do you notice first?
What do you think this is?
What do you think you’re supposed to do?
What feels clear?
What feels confusing?
What questions would you have?

You aren’t asking AI whether the design is good.

You’re asking what happens when someone encounters it.

Then look for the gap.

Compare the response to what you intended.

Where did the interpretation match?

Where did it diverge?

What did you assume would be obvious?

Those gaps are useful.

They give you somewhere to look.

AI isn’t your audience.

It’s a way of rehearsing the experience of being someone who isn’t you.
