AI For Design Thinking — AI as a Devils Advocate

AI For Design Thinking: A collection of small experiments for using AI as part of the design process — it doesn’t have to make the thing, it can help you see the thing.

Use AI to argue against your idea.

You’ve spent time developing a concept.

Now try to break it.

Give AI your idea and ask it to find the problems.

What assumptions is this concept making?
Who might this not work for?
What could go wrong?
What is the weakest part of the argument?
What would someone who strongly disagrees say?

Don’t defend yourself yet.

Let it attack the idea.

Then separate the useful criticism from the noise.

Some objections will be irrelevant.

Some will be wrong.

And occasionally, something will sting a little because it is true.

That’s useful.

A good concept doesn’t need to survive every criticism.

But you should know which criticisms you’re choosing to ignore—and why.

Don’t use AI to validate your idea.

Try using it to make your idea harder to defend.

Still figuring it out.
