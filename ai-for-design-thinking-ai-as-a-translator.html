AI as a Translator

AI For Design Thinking: A collection of small experiments for using AI as something a designer can think with during the design process.

Use AI to see whether your idea survives a change in language.

Take your concept.

Explain it to AI.

Then ask it to translate the idea for someone else.

Explain this to a five-year-old.
Explain it to someone who knows nothing about the subject.
Explain it to an expert.
Explain it without using design terminology.
Explain it in one sentence.

Now look at what changed.

What disappeared?

What became more important?

What did you leave out?

What did you assume everyone already understood?

Translation can reveal the structure underneath an idea.

If your concept only makes sense when you explain everything around it, there may be something worth examining.

Don’t use translation to make your idea simpler.

Use it to find out what your idea actually is.
