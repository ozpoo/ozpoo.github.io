AI as a Devils Advocate

Argue against your idea

AI for Design Thinking: Practices for using AI within the design process.

What it is

Use AI to construct a serious argument against a direction you currently believe in.

Works well with

  • Strong concepts
  • Design decisions
  • Proposals
  • Strategies
  • Research conclusions
  • Assumptions

The question

If my idea is wrong, why might it be wrong?

Try it

  1. State your position clearly.
  2. Ask AI to argue against it.
  3. Require specific evidence or reasoning.
  4. Ask it to identify the strongest objection.
  5. Respond to the objection yourself.
  6. Revise your idea if necessary.
  7. Record what changed.

Look for

  • Blind spots
  • Weak assumptions
  • Alternative interpretations
  • Unconsidered consequences
  • Evidence you need

Case study

Defending a design direction

You believe a deliberately confusing interface will make users more curious.

Ask AI:

Make the strongest possible argument against this design strategy. Focus on usability, accessibility, user expectations, and unintended consequences.

The goal is not to win the argument.

The goal is to find out whether your idea can survive one.
