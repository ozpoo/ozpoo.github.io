AI For Design Thinking
AI as a Metaphor Machine

AI For Design Thinking: A collection of small experiments for using AI as part of the design process — it doesn’t have to make the thing, it can help you see the thing.

Use AI to find a different way into a problem.

Describe what you’re working on.

Then ask AI for metaphors.

Not slogans.

Not names.

Not finished concepts.

Metaphors.

What could this project be like?
Give me 20 unexpected metaphors for this problem.
Avoid obvious comparisons.

Maybe your interface becomes a doorway.

Your archive becomes a garden.

Your identity system becomes a language.

Your messy collection of information becomes a city.

The metaphor isn’t the solution.

It’s a change in perspective.

Once you’ve found one that feels interesting, leave AI behind.

Draw it.

Write about it.

Make something from it.

Ask yourself:

What does this metaphor let me see that I couldn’t see before?

A good metaphor doesn’t explain the answer.

It changes the question.
