AI as a Critique Partner

Make your design argue with itself

AI for Design Thinking: Practices for using AI within the design process.

Part of The Foundations: An emerging body of research, teaching, and public inquiry into technological literacy and human agency.

What it is

Use AI to examine a design decision from multiple critical perspectives.

The question

What might be wrong with this?

Works well with

  • Concepts
  • Design rationales
  • Visual systems
  • Written statements
  • Presentations
  • Early proposals
  • Finished work

Try it

  1. Describe your design and its intended purpose.
  2. Explain the audience and context.
  3. Ask AI to identify strengths and weaknesses.
  4. Ask it to challenge your assumptions.
  5. Ask what might be confusing or ineffective.
  6. Separate useful criticism from generic feedback.
  7. Decide what deserves further investigation.
  8. Revise or defend your decisions.

Look for

  • Unclear assumptions
  • Contradictions
  • Missing information
  • Audience problems
  • Unintended interpretations
  • Weak reasoning

Case study

Critiquing an information graphic

Give AI the structure and rationale for an information graphic.

Ask:
What might a first-time viewer misunderstand?

Then:
What assumptions does this design make?

Then:
What information appears more important than it actually is?

The AI is not deciding whether the design is successful.

It is helping you interrogate your own decisions.
