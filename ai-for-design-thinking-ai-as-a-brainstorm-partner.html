AI For Design Thinking — AI as a Brainstorm Partner

AI For Design Thinking: A collection of small experiments for using AI as part of the design process — it doesn’t have to make the thing, it can help you see the thing.

Use AI to make more possibilities.

Sometimes the hardest part of ideation isn’t having an idea.

It’s having enough ideas to compare.

Give AI the problem you’re working on.

Then ask it to generate possibilities — but don’t ask for the answer.

Give me 20 different directions this could go.
Make them meaningfully different from one another.
Don’t refine them yet.

Now resist the temptation to pick the best one.

Look for the strange ones.

The boring ones.

The ones that are almost useful.

The ones that make you think:

Wait. What if...

That’s the point.

AI can produce possibilities much faster than you can evaluate them.

Use that difference.

Generate broadly. Evaluate yourself.

The value isn’t necessarily in anything AI gives you.

It might be in the idea that its idea gives you.

Still figuring it out.
