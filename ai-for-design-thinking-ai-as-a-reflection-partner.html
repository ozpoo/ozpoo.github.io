AI For Design Thinking — AI as a Reflection Partner

AI For Design Thinking: A collection of small experiments for using AI as part of the design process — it doesn’t have to make the thing, it can help you see the thing.

Use AI to look back at your own process.

Don’t give AI the finished project.

Give it the messy stuff.

Your notes.

Your discarded ideas.

Your questions.

Your iterations.

Your frustrations.

Then ask:

What patterns do you notice in how I’m thinking?
What questions keep coming back?
What seems to be changing?
What am I repeatedly avoiding?
What assumptions seem to be guiding my decisions?

Don’t ask:

What should I do?

Ask:

What am I doing?

That’s a very different question.

AI can be surprisingly useful at noticing patterns across a body of material.

But the interpretation still belongs to you.

Maybe the pattern means something.

Maybe it doesn’t.

Maybe the most interesting thing is that you didn’t notice it yourself.

Use AI as a mirror, not an authority.

Sometimes seeing your own thinking from the outside is enough to move it forward.
