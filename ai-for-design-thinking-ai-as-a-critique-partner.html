AI For Design Thinking — AI as a Critique Partner

AI For Design Thinking: A collection of small experiments for using AI as part of the design process — it doesn’t have to make the thing, it can help you see the thing.

Use AI to make your design argue with itself.

Show AI your work.

Then give it a role.

Ask it to respond as:

  • an art director
  • a first-time viewer
  • a potential user
  • someone unfamiliar with the subject
  • someone encountering the work in a specific context

Ask for observations, not solutions.

What is working?
What isn’t clear?
What feels intentional?
What feels accidental?
Where does the design lose you?
What assumptions does this design make?

Then do something important:

Don’t immediately fix anything.

Look at the responses.

Some will be useful.

Some will be wrong.

Some will reveal something you hadn’t considered.

Some will simply confirm what you already knew.

That’s the exercise.

Take the responses that create a productive tension with your own thinking.

Then decide what — if anything — you want to change.

The goal isn’t better AI feedback.

It’s better questions about your own work.

Critique gives you perspectives.

You decide what to do with them.
