AI For Design Thinking — AI As a User Tester

AI For Design Thinking: A collection of small experiments for using AI as part of the design process — it doesn’t have to make the thing, it can help you see the thing.

Use AI to rehearse an interaction before you build it.

You have a prototype.

Maybe it’s a website.

An app.

A poster.

An interface.

A sign.

Something people are supposed to understand or use.

Give AI the context.

Then give it a person.

You are a first-time user.
You have never seen this interface before.
Your goal is to ______.

Now walk through the experience.

Ask:

What do you notice?
What do you expect to happen?
What would you click?
What would you ignore?
Where might you hesitate?
What would you misunderstand?

You aren’t testing whether AI can predict your users.

It can’t.

You’re using it to practice looking at your design from somewhere other than your own perspective.

Then test the same thing with an actual person.

Compare the two.

The differences are interesting too.

A simulation isn’t a substitute for a user.

It’s a way to find questions worth asking one.
