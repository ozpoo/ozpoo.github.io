AI as a Translator

See whether an idea survives a change in language

What it is

Use AI to translate a concept between languages, levels of complexity, formats, or modes of communication.

Works well with

  • Concepts
  • Statements
  • Instructions
  • Research
  • Presentations
  • Visual ideas
  • Technical information

The question

What survives when I change how this idea is expressed?

Try it

  1. State your idea clearly.
  2. Ask AI to express it differently.
  3. Change the audience, language, tone, scale, or format.
  4. Compare the versions.
  5. Identify what remains consistent.
  6. Identify what changes.
  7. Ask whether the difference matters.
  8. Refine your original idea.

Look for

  • Core ideas
  • Ambiguity
  • Missing meaning
  • Unnecessary complexity
  • Assumptions
  • Language-dependent concepts

Case study

Translating a design concept

Start with:

This identity system represents movement through a city.

Ask AI to explain the idea to:

  • A five-year-old
  • A city planner
  • A graphic designer
  • A visitor
  • Someone who has never seen the project

If the explanation changes dramatically, investigate why.

The exercise tests whether the idea itself is clear—or whether it depends on specialized language.
