AI as a Simulator

Rehearse how a concept might behave

AI for Design Thinking: Practices for using AI within the design process.

Part of The Foundations: An emerging body of research, teaching, and public inquiry into technological literacy and human agency.

What it is

Use AI to simulate possible situations, contexts, or responses before building or testing the actual design.

The question

What might happen if…?

Works well with

  • Services
  • Interfaces
  • Campaigns
  • Systems
  • Experiences
  • Scenarios
  • Interactions

Try it

  1. Describe the design and context.
  2. Define the situation you want to explore.
  3. Establish the user’s role or perspective.
  4. Ask AI to simulate the scenario.
  5. Introduce complications.
  6. Test alternative decisions.
  7. Identify where the concept breaks down.
  8. Test important findings with real people when possible.

Look for

  • Unexpected situations
  • Failure points
  • Edge cases
  • Different responses
  • Conflicting needs
  • Unintended consequences

Case study

Simulating a service experience

Imagine you are designing a system for checking into an unfamiliar building.

Ask AI to simulate:

  • A first-time visitor
  • Someone in a hurry
  • Someone who does not speak the primary language
  • Someone arriving after hours

The simulation can reveal questions worth testing.

It cannot replace testing with actual people.
