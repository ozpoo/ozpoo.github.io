AI as a Metaphor Machine

Find a different way into a problem

AI for Design Thinking: Practices for using AI within the design process.

Part of The Foundations: An emerging body of research, teaching, and public inquiry into technological literacy and human agency.

What it is

Use AI to generate metaphors, analogies, and conceptual comparisons that allow you to look at a problem from another angle.

The question

What else is this like?

Works well with

  • Abstract concepts
  • Complex systems
  • Communication problems
  • Branding
  • Concept development
  • Visual language

Try it

  1. Describe the problem.
  2. Ask AI for unrelated systems or situations that behave similarly.
  3. Look for surprising comparisons.
  4. Identify what the metaphor reveals.
  5. Identify where the metaphor breaks down.
  6. Translate the useful part into your design problem.

Look for

  • New conceptual language
  • Unexpected relationships
  • Visual possibilities
  • Useful contradictions
  • Better questions

Case study

Understanding information overload

Ask:
What systems behave like an overwhelmed person trying to process too much information?

Possible metaphors might include:

  • A crowded intersection
  • An overloaded electrical circuit
  • A kitchen during service
  • A library without a catalog
  • A river during a flood

The metaphor does not solve the problem.

It gives you another way to see it.
