AI as an Audience

Step outside your own perspective

What it is

Use AI to explore how the same design might be interpreted by different audiences.

Works well with

  • Communication
  • Campaigns
  • Exhibitions
  • Identity
  • Information design
  • Public-facing work
  • Writing

The question

How might someone else experience this?

Try it

  1. Describe your design and intended audience.
  2. Identify several possible audience perspectives.
  3. Ask AI to respond from each perspective.
  4. Compare the responses.
  5. Identify differences.
  6. Identify assumptions built into the design.
  7. Decide what needs real-world testing.

Look for

  • Different interpretations
  • Different priorities
  • Unfamiliar assumptions
  • Accessibility concerns
  • Cultural or contextual differences
  • Questions your intended audience might ask

Case study

Seeing an exhibition through different audiences

Ask AI to respond to an exhibition concept as:

  • A first-time visitor
  • A subject-matter expert
  • A teenager
  • A parent
  • Someone unfamiliar with the topic

The goal is not to determine what every audience member will think.

It is to practice leaving your own point of view.
