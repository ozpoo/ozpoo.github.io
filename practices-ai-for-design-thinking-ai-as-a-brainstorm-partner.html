AI as a Brainstorm Partner

Make more possibilities

AI for Design Thinking: Practices for using AI within the design process.

What it is

Use AI to generate possibilities quickly so you can explore a wider range of directions before deciding what to pursue.

Works well with

  • Early concepts
  • Naming
  • Visual directions
  • Questions
  • Metaphors
  • Features
  • Approaches
  • Alternative solutions

The question

What else could this be?

Try it

  1. Describe the problem you are exploring.
  2. Give AI the constraints you already know.
  3. Ask for multiple possibilities.
  4. Reject the first obvious ideas.
  5. Ask for stranger, riskier, or more unexpected directions.
  6. Combine ideas that interest you.
  7. Develop your own variations.
  8. Stop generating when you have enough material to think with.

Look for

  • Unexpected directions
  • Variations
  • Contradictions
  • Ideas worth developing
  • Ideas worth rejecting
  • Questions you had not considered

Case study

Generating directions for an exhibition

Instead of asking:

Design an exhibition poster.

Ask:

I am designing a poster for an exhibition about abandoned infrastructure. Generate 20 different conceptual directions. Avoid generic imagery of buildings or decay. Explore ideas involving memory, absence, scale, time, and evidence.

The value is not the 20 answers.

The value may be the three ideas you would not have thought to consider.
