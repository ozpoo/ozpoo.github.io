AI as a Reflection Partner

Look back at your own process

What it is

Use AI to examine your process after the fact and help identify decisions, changes, patterns, and assumptions.

Works well with

  • Process journals
  • Sketchbooks
  • Iterations
  • Critique notes
  • Failed experiments
  • Project documentation
  • Reflection writing

The question

What happened while I was making this?

Try it

  1. Gather your process documentation.
  2. Give AI the relevant material.
  3. Ask it to summarize the evolution of your thinking.
  4. Ask what decisions changed.
  5. Ask what patterns appear in your process.
  6. Compare its reading with your own.
  7. Identify what you learned.
  8. Record what you would do differently.

Look for

  • Repeated decisions
  • Abandoned ideas
  • Turning points
  • Assumptions
  • Habits
  • Productive failures

Case study

Looking back at a project

Give AI your early sketches, notes, and final rationale.

Ask:

What changed between my initial idea and final direction?

Then:

Where does the process appear to have shifted?

The purpose is not to have AI write your reflection.

It is to help you see your own process from a distance.
