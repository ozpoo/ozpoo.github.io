AI as a Pattern Finder

Look across information for recurring ideas

What it is

Use AI to compare a collection of references, observations, notes, or iterations and identify recurring themes or characteristics.

Works well with

  • Research notes
  • Interviews
  • References
  • Design iterations
  • Feedback
  • Images
  • Text collections
  • Observations

The question

What keeps showing up?

Try it

  1. Gather the material.
  2. Give AI the relevant context.
  3. Ask it to identify recurring themes or characteristics.
  4. Ask it to group related observations.
  5. Look for patterns you recognize.
  6. Look for patterns you do not recognize.
  7. Verify the patterns against the original material.
  8. Decide which patterns matter.

Look for

  • Recurring concepts
  • Repeated language
  • Common characteristics
  • Outliers
  • Contradictions
  • Emerging themes

Case study

Finding patterns in design references

Collect 20 visual references.

Ask AI to identify recurring characteristics in:

  • Composition
  • Typography
  • Color
  • Scale
  • Image treatment
  • Tone
  • Spatial relationships

Then compare its observations against your own.

The useful discovery may not be the pattern AI identifies.

It may be the difference between what you thought you were looking at and what you were actually collecting.
