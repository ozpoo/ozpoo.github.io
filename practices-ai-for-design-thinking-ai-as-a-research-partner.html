AI as a Research Partner

Identify questions, assumptions, connections, and things to investigate

What it is

Use AI to expand a research process by identifying questions and connections that deserve further investigation.

Works well with

  • Early research
  • Complex topics
  • Unfamiliar subjects
  • Literature searches
  • Background research
  • Concept development

The question

What don’t I know yet?

Try it

  1. Describe what you currently know.
  2. Identify what you are trying to understand.
  3. Ask AI what questions you should investigate.
  4. Ask what assumptions you may be making.
  5. Ask what concepts or subjects are connected.
  6. Identify claims that require verification.
  7. Research the most useful questions using appropriate sources.
  8. Record what you learned.

Look for

  • Missing questions
  • Hidden assumptions
  • Related subjects
  • Contradictions
  • Vocabulary
  • Leads for further research

Case study

Investigating public transportation

Rather than asking AI:

Tell me about public transportation.

Ask:

I am investigating why people choose not to use public transportation. What questions should I investigate before forming a conclusion?

The response becomes a research map, not a final answer.
