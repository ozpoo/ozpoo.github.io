AI as a User Tester

Rehearse an interaction before you build it

What it is

Use AI to simulate a possible user interaction with a product, interface, service, or system.

Works well with

  • Interfaces
  • Websites
  • Apps
  • Forms
  • Instructions
  • Services
  • Interactive systems

The question

What might someone do when they encounter this?

Try it

  1. Describe the intended user.
  2. Describe the context.
  3. Present the design or interaction.
  4. Ask AI to respond as the user.
  5. Ask what the user notices first.
  6. Ask what they expect to happen.
  7. Introduce confusion or interruptions.
  8. Record questions worth testing with real users.

Look for

  • Expectations
  • Confusion
  • Missing information
  • Friction
  • Misinterpretation
  • Unexpected behaviors

Case study

Testing an interface

Describe a website and its navigation.

Ask AI to act as someone trying to find a specific piece of information.

Then ask:

Tell me what you would click first and why.

The result is not evidence that a real user would behave that way.

It is a rehearsal that can help you identify what to test.
